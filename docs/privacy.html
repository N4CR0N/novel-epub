Privacy policy

WebNovel Downloader & Translator · last updated 15 November 2026

WebNovel Downloader & Translator is a free, open-source app that runs entirely on your own computer or phone. There is no account with us and no server of ours: we do not collect, receive or store any of your data.

Google user data

  • What the app accesses. Only if you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. With it the app can create a folder in your Google Drive and read, update and delete only the files and folders it created itself. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive. It does not request your contacts, email, calendar or any other Google data.
  • How it is used. The app uploads the books you finish or choose to upload, and a backup of your library (downloaded chapters, translations, glossary and story notes), so that you can read them and continue on your other devices where you sign in with the same account.
  • Where it is stored. These files are stored in your own Google Drive, under your control. The sign-in token is stored only on your device (the Windows Credential Manager on a computer, the app's private storage on Android).
  • Sharing. Your Google data is never sent to us or to anyone else, never sold, and never used for advertising or for training AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Removing access. Sign out in the app, or remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The files in your Drive stay until you delete them.

Other services you choose

To download a book, the app connects to the novel site whose link you give. To translate, it sends the chapter text to the AI provider you set up, with your own API key, under that provider's terms. Nothing else leaves your device.
