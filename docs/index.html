WebNovel Downloader & Translator

Free and open source · Windows and Android

Give the app a web novel link or a file (EPUB, PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD, HTML). It downloads the chapters, checks them, optionally translates them with the AI provider you choose (Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Groq and others, using your own API key) and builds a single e-book: EPUB by default, or Word, HTML, TXT or Markdown.

Google Drive

If you sign in with Google, the app creates its own folder in your Google Drive and uploads your finished books and a backup of your library there, so you can read them and continue on your other devices. It uses the narrow drive.file permission: it can only see the files it created itself, not the rest of your Drive.
